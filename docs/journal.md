## 2026-10-03

- Started the project. Goal: a finished, well-documented portfolio piece that I also use daily.
- Idea: a habit tracker where distracting apps stay blocked until a habit is done, proven by scanning an NFC tag placed where the habit happens.
- Constraints: Windows, no Mac, Android phone (Galaxy A15). Android first, iOS later.
- Clarified: the app tracks many habits (daily ones like skincare or bedtime, weekly ones like the gym, measurable ones like a 10 km run). NFC is a feature, not the whole app. It's what has worked best for me so far.
- Named it **Déclic**. Keystone was the other favorite, but two habit apps already use it.
- Repo is public with no license: readable by recruiters, all rights reserved in case I sell it later.
- Docs in English, the working language of the field. The app UI can be bilingual.
- Researched the architect role and the platform. Notes in `research.md`, plan in `ROADMAP.md`.
